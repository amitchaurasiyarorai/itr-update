ITR Update

Income Tax e-Filing portal statistics, tracked every day

Why this exists

The e-Filing portal shows only today's totals. ITR Update keeps the daily history, so you can see how fast returns are being processed and whether the backlog is shrinking.

How it works

The portal's public statistics are fetched twice a day (8 AM and 6 PM IST), saved to an open CSV, and charted here. No backend, no tracking.

What's tracked

Registered users
Individuals registered on the e-Filing portal
Returns filed
ITRs filed this assessment year
E-verified
Returns verified and in the processing queue
Processed
Returns the department has processed
Backlog
E-verified minus processed

Independent project, not affiliated with the Income Tax Department. For official information, visit incometax.gov.in.
